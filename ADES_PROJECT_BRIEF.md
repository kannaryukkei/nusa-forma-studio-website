## 5. Exploration and decision
- Research pack: IN_PROGRESS; must be completed and frozen before R1. Research notes below are not a claim of exhaustive inspection.
- Directly inspected:
  - Sudut Ruang Arsitek, https://sudutruang.com/ (homepage crawl): multi-service navigation, services, project examples, team, testimonial and enquiry form; broad service breadth and trust claims create a high content/proof burden. Observed crawl includes questionable duplicated/template artefacts and login/shop/checkout links; inspect live behaviour before any reuse.
  - Beta Design Studio, https://betadesain.com/ (homepage crawl): deep hierarchy, project taxonomy, manufacturing/process content, portfolio filters, contact form. Strong service/process/portfolio connection, but multiple numeric, certification, award and client proof elements are unavailable to this fictional brand and must not be imitated.
  - Yona Design Studio, https://yonadesignstudio.com/ (homepage crawl): compact navigation across projects/products/news/team, research-led studio narrative and timeline; restrained storytelling, but its scale and project/client proof are not transferable.
  - WS Interior Studio, https://www.wsinteriorstudio.com/ (homepage crawl): services, projects, about, collaboration; the crawl exposes generic placeholder copy in service cards, an anti-pattern for content completeness.
  - H-Studio case study, https://www.h-studio.id/en/case-studies/forma-architecture-studio: structured portfolio/editorial/expertise architecture, page hierarchy, tokens, responsive/media optimisation and adaptive navigation are described; this is a case-study source, not independent proof of results.
- Search-discovered local/market references (not all directly inspected): ARSENT https://www.arsentdesign.com/ (one-studio design/build/manufacture positioning); SATU https://www.surabayainteriordesign.com/ (project-led Surabaya interior positioning); TWJ https://twjinterior.com/ (3D visualisation niche); WS Interior, Beta, Yona and Sudut Ruang above.
- Local discovery also surfaced nearby Surabaya-West businesses including Woodenly Interior, Java Interior, iDekore and Griya Laras. Business listings are not endorsements or quality rankings; use only as market-context leads.
- Initial lessons: (1) show scope boundaries and process alongside imagery; (2) portfolio is a core buying proof, but synthetic concepts need a different visual label and cannot substitute for real work; (3) custom furniture is a meaningful differentiator only if production responsibility is clearly stated; (4) avoid unsupported metrics, certification, testimonial, response-time and end-to-end execution claims; (5) make the contact path honest when no real endpoint exists; (6) ensure service cards contain complete, useful copy.
- Initial reuse/adapt/avoid: reuse a clear service taxonomy, project-story structure, process explanation and contextual CTA; adapt to a smaller fictional studio and fewer routes; avoid competitor copy, logos, imagery, layouts, testimonials, metrics, claims, copied code and unnecessary complexity.
