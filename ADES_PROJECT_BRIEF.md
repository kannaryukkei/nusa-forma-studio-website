### C. Synthesis — patterns, anti-patterns and design opportunities
- Adopt: a crisp route/navigation hierarchy; service descriptions with explicit inclusions/exclusions; process stages with named outputs and approval points; project storytelling with typology/context and clear image captions; low-friction contextual inquiry preparation; reusable layout tokens and components; semantic links/buttons and responsive media.
- Challenge: generic “premium” studio hero claims, repeated metric strips, undifferentiated service-card grids, giant nav taxonomies, fake project counters, overbuilt CMS/editorial architecture, ornamental scroll effects and conversion overlays.
- Avoid: unverified awards/certifications/team credentials/years/metrics/testimonials; implying generated visuals are built projects; claiming in-house manufacture or turnkey construction; dead contact endpoints; placeholder service copy; copied competitor layouts/copy/code/media.
- Nusa Forma-specific opportunity: make the distinction between **design**, **custom furniture**, and **optional implementation coordination** unusually clear; use the two synthetic concepts as labelled demonstrations of thinking rather than proof; show how a visitor moves from space problem → relevant service → process/decision checkpoint → prepared inquiry.
- Conversion principle: primary CTA remains “Konsultasikan Proyek Anda” but honestly opens a local-only preparation state, with visible disclosure that nothing is sent or saved. No fabricated WhatsApp handoff.
- Motion principle: interaction should clarify hierarchy or state; keep motion restrained and respect reduced-motion. No carousel/autoplay required.
- Responsive principle: treat narrow, medium/tablet and wide layouts as designed compositions; reserve image aspect ratios; allow typography to scale with readable bounds and content.
- Reference inspection limitation: no browser automation, screenshot capture or live interaction testing. Page content was inspected via accessible page extraction/search; actual sticky/menu/hover/focus/touch/animation/viewport states remain NOT_VERIFIED. No claim of browser QA or visual screenshot pass.
- Asset rights: none of the reference-site assets may be reused. No asset source has yet been approved for Nusa Forma; use original CSS/SVG diagrams or generated media with provenance and visible concept disclosure. If generated imagery is used, record tool/model if exposed and do not imply documentary truth.
